| Key       | Action                              |

| `L`       | Fire red laser (damages enemies and boss) |

## Notes

### Anti-Nuclear Shield (O Key)
- **Anti-bomb shield (O)** consumes one anti-bomb item per use.  
- Collect glowing red/yellow anti-bomb orbs across stages 1–10.  
- You need at least **5** to survive all nuclear attacks in the boss fight (Stage 11).
- The boss triggers nuclear attacks both at HP thresholds AND every 30 seconds.

### Bombs
- **B2 Bomb (1)** is limited to **3 total** across the whole game – use wisely!  
- **Arrow Bomb (2)** has **20 total** available across all stages.  
- **Halo Bomb (3)** has **2 total** across the whole game.

### Trump Transformation
- The trump transformation (triggered by collecting a golden **Activation Orb**) lasts **20 seconds** and grants:
  - Immunity to enemy touch
  - Special visual glow effect
- It is **not** activated by a key – it activates automatically on pickup.

### Extra Enemies (Blue Soldiers)
- Starting from the regular stages, you'll encounter **Extra Enemies** (blue soldiers with boxes).
- They have **3 HP** each and fire **3 blue laser shots** at you.
- Number of Extra Enemies per stage depends on your chosen difficulty.
- Their shots are blocked by platforms, so use cover strategically!

### Boss Fight (Stage 11)
- The boss has **500 HP** (50 laser hits to defeat).
- The boss fires volleys of blue/green orbs that get more frequent as HP drops.
- **Nuclear attacks** happen at specific HP thresholds AND every 30 seconds.
- When nuclear mode activates:
  - Screen flashes red
  - 5-second countdown begins
  - Press **O** immediately to activate shield
  - No shield = you take a hit
- In addition to the boss, you'll face **shooting soldiers** based on difficulty:
  - Easy: 2 soldiers
  - Middle: 4 soldiers  
  - Hard: 10 soldiers

### ML Dashboard & Statistics
- Press **M** on the Game Over or Victory screen to open the **ML Dashboard**.
- View detailed statistics, performance analysis, and machine learning insights.
- The dashboard shows:
  - Score predictions
  - Performance metrics
  - Feature importance analysis
  - Historical data and trends

### Saving Your Score
- Press **S** on the Game Over or Victory screen to save your score to the database.
- Your score is calculated based on:
  - Enemies killed
  - Extra enemies eliminated
  - Stages completed
  - Survival time
  - Final outcome (victory/defeat)

## Device Selection

On first launch, you'll be asked to choose:
- **PC / Computer (1)** – Standard keyboard controls
- **Mobile / Phone (2)** – On-screen touch controls

You can select with mouse click or by pressing **1** or **2** on your keyboard.

## Tips

1. **Collect Energy Spheres** early – they permanently increase your max stamina.
2. **Save anti-bombs** for the boss fight – you need at least 5 to be safe.
3. **Use B2 Bombs strategically** – you only get 3 for the entire game.
4. **Watch the timer** – each stage has a 4-minute time limit.
5. **Trump Mode** makes you invincible – use it to pass dangerous sections.
6. **Cover is key** against Extra Enemies – their shots are blocked by platforms.
7. **In boss fight** – focus on dodging while dealing damage. Don't forget about the shooting soldiers!
