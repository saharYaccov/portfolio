## **Government Roles & Tenure Analytics**
---

## 🧠 **Overview**
The **Government Roles & Tenure in Israel Dashboard** provides an **interactive, data-driven exploration** of Israeli politicians' careers, focusing on their **government roles, tenure length, academic background, and military service**. This dashboard is designed for **political analysts, researchers, and data enthusiasts** who want to uncover insights into the correlation between politicians' backgrounds and their success in government positions.

The data is sourced from **public records and Wikipedia**, and the dashboard is built using **Tableau** for dynamic visualizations and analysis.

---

## 🔧 **Features**
- **Interactive Exploration**: Filter politicians by **party, role category (e.g., economic, security), tenure length, and academic/military background**.
- **Comprehensive Visualizations**: Bar charts, line graphs, and heatmaps to visualize **tenure trends, role distribution, and background correlations**.
- **Tenure Analytics**: Track **average tenure length, role transitions, and career progression** over time.
- **Background Insights**: Analyze the impact of **academic degrees (type, field, institution) and military rank** on political success.
- **Comparative Analysis**: Compare politicians' careers based on **education, military service, and party affiliation**.

---

## 🛠 **Tools & Technologies**
- **Tableau Desktop / Tableau Public**: Interactive dashboards and visual storytelling.
- **Calculated Fields & Parameters**: Custom KPIs for tenure length, role categories, and background metrics.
- **Data Sources**: Excel, CSV, and web-scraped data from Wikipedia and public records.

---

## 📈 **Use Cases**
- **Political Analysis**: Study the correlation between **education, military service, and political success**.
- **Career Tracking**: Monitor politicians' **tenure length, role transitions, and career progression**.
- **Data Storytelling**: Communicate insights about **Israeli political leadership** through interactive visuals.
- **Research & Reporting**: Support academic or journalistic projects with **data-driven insights**.

---

## 📌 **Notes**
- Requires **Tableau Desktop** or access to **Tableau Public** for full interactivity.
- Optimized for **filtering, drill-down, and comparative analysis**.
- Data is sourced from **public records and Wikipedia**; refresh periodically for updates.
