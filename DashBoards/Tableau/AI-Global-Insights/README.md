## 🧠 Overview
The **AI-Market-Pulse** dashboard provides a multi-dimensional, interactive analysis of the global Artificial Intelligence sector. This project explores the intersection between AI service density, workforce scalability, and economic indicators across countries and major tech hubs. Using Tableau's advanced visual analytics, it allows stakeholders to identify high-value market trends and workforce distribution patterns.

## 🔧 Features
- **Global & Regional Insights:** Visualize AI service distribution across leading nations (UK, India, CA, Australia) and specific regions like Israel.
- **Salary Benchmarking:** Detailed analysis of hourly wages, comparing minimum and maximum salary ranges within the AI field.
- **Workforce Analytics:** Explore the relationship between workforce size and service integration in high-paying companies ($30+/hour).
- **Urban Leaderboard:** A granular view of AI service density in major global cities, including London, New York, and Tel Aviv.
- **Custom UI Design:** Integrated tech-focused background with circuit-board aesthetics for an immersive data experience.

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- **Tableau Desktop:** For advanced data visualization and dashboard construction.
- **Advanced Mapping & Bar Charts:** To represent geographical and urban service volume.
- **Calculated KPIs:** Real-time tracking of average workers, hourly salaries, and service ratios.

## 📈 Use Cases
- **Market Research:** Understand which countries and cities are leading in AI service adoption.
- **Financial Planning:** Analyze salary benchmarks to understand labor costs in the global AI market.
- **Strategic Expansion:** Identify emerging tech hubs for resource allocation or talent scouting.

## 📌 Notes

- The dashboard is optimized for deep-dive filtering by country, city, and company pay grade.
