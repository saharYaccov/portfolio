## 🧠 Overview
The **Football IL Dashboard** provides an in-depth, data-driven exploration of the **Israeli Premier League**.  
It enables analysts, data enthusiasts, and football fans to explore **match statistics, performance trends, and comparative analytics** across teams and players from the 2024/25 season (2 Oct – 29 Sep).

Built in **Tableau**, this dashboard integrates data collected from league games to visualize insights such as goals, cards, and shooting accuracy.  

---

## 🔧 Features
- **Interactive Dashboards** – Filter by team, round, or player to explore KPIs.  
- **Comparative Analysis** – Evaluate team strengths and weaknesses across matches.  
- **Performance Metrics** – Includes goals, shots on/off target, yellow/red cards, and more.  
- **Trend Insights** – Visualizes progression of key indicators across the season.  
- **Clean Design** – Optimized for clarity, accessibility, and storytelling.  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- **Tableau Public** – Dashboard visualization & interactivity.  
- **Excel / CSV** – Match and player-level datasets.  
- **Python (Pandas, NumPy)** – Data preprocessing and transformations.  
- **Sports Data Sources** – Israeli Premier League match statistics.  

---

## 📈 Use Cases
- **Sports Analytics** – Analyze and compare team performance across rounds.  
- **Coaching Insights** – Identify tactical patterns and performance fluctuations.  
- **Fan Engagement** – Present visually compelling league statistics.  
- **Data Storytelling** – Combine analytics and visualization to convey football insights.  

---

## 📌 Notes
- Dashboard available in **`.twb`** (Tableau Workbook) and **`.pdf`** formats for review.  
- Requires **Tableau Public** to explore interactively.  
- Data covers the **Israeli Premier League** season from **2 October to 29 September**.
